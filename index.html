Is shadowbanning real? We're experiencing a sudden drop in views after the update.

Reach

Instagram doesn't try to silently silence accounts, but it can put accounts on restrictions. If you're restricted, you can see that under Profile, Settings, Account status, including whether content was taken down or you're no longer recommended to non-followers. They don't try to shadowban people without telling them, but they do restrict accounts in certain circumstances and let you know.

Which analytic is most crucial for reach on reels?

Reach

The three metrics that matter most for Reels reach are like rate (the percentage of people who saw your Reel and liked it), send rate (the percentage who saw it and sent it to a friend), and watch time (how much of the Reel they watched). Sends matter more for unconnected reach, or people who don't follow you, and likes matter more for connected reach, or people who do.

Does upgrading a trial reel to followers stop reach to non followers?

Reach

No. Once you upgrade a trial Reel to a Reel that's on your profile and goes to your followers, it is still eligible to be shown to non-followers. Recommendations don't stop at that point.

Do following counts matter anymore?

Reach

Counts still matter, and more than on some platforms such as TikTok, but less than they used to because much of creators' reach now comes from recommendations to non-followers. He argues that recommending posts from accounts people do not follow grows overall engagement and gives the average creator more reach, while acknowledging that this is contentious.
