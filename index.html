What if I edit my video with CapCut?Reels & video

No problem. Instagram does not demote content made in other apps, as long as there is no watermark. A visible logo advertising another app gets less distribution. He encourages trying Edits, which is about to launch on desktop.

There is work to do on Android, and on every app. Most Instagram users are on Android, so it needs to be great. Report issues and the team will try to fix them quickly.

Why did so many talented IG employees leave this year?Company

AI is causing anxiety across tech, people are rethinking their roles, and the talent wars are out of control, so more people are moving. Instagram lost some great people, worked hard to keep its best, and also hired many strong people.
